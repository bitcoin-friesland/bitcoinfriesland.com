What’s in it for you

Why Bitcoin is interesting for your business

Accepting Bitcoin is more than an extra payment method. It brings new customers and extra visibility, at low cost.

New customers

Bitcoiners actively look for places where they can pay with bitcoin, including when they travel. Accepting it makes your business stand out to them.

On the Bitcoin map

Bitcoiners find your business on the Bitcoin Friesland map, and "Pay here with Bitcoin" stickers on your door show it right away.

Word of mouth

Bitcoiners like to share new places with each other, online and at meetups.

Low fees

A Lightning payment usually costs a fraction of a cent, far less than the cost of many card payments.

Instant and final

The money arrives within seconds, evenings and weekends included. A bitcoin payment is final: there are no chargebacks like with credit cards.

Start small

You can start with a free app on your phone or tablet. You decide whether to keep the bitcoin you receive or convert it to euros.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bitcoin sustainable?

The energy impact of Bitcoin mining depends heavily on the electricity source. Mining can use renewable energy and surplus electricity that would otherwise be difficult to use. It is therefore useful to consider the energy source, not only total consumption.

How do I account for Bitcoin?

For tax purposes, Bitcoin is generally treated as an asset in the Netherlands. If you receive Bitcoin as payment, you should record the euro value at the time of transaction. Consult with your accountant about the specific requirements for your business.

How do I use it at my register?

Most businesses use a simple mobile app for Bitcoin payments. Solutions like Coinos, BTCPay Server, or Wallet of Satoshi let you generate a QR code that customers scan. These transactions happen in seconds and are verified immediately on the Lightning Network.

How do I start accepting Bitcoin?

The easiest way to start is with a simple wallet app like Coinos.io. It takes just a few minutes to set up, and you can begin accepting both on-chain Bitcoin and Lightning payments immediately. Make sure to add your business to the Bitcoin map after you start accepting it!

Important: Tax legislation can change. Always consult a certified accountant or tax advisor.
