# About Bitcoin Friesland

Bitcoin Friesland is an independent volunteer community for Bitcoin education, adoption and meetups in Friesland, the Netherlands. Beginners, experienced users and businesses can learn together and share their experiences.

## Frequently asked questions

- **What does Bitcoin Friesland do?** We bring people together at Bitcoin meetups and events, share practical knowledge and help visitors find places that accept Bitcoin.
- **Can I join for free?** Yes. Participation in the community and the Telegram and Signal groups is free. Becoming a supporter is optional. Individual events may have their own ticket costs.
- **How do I get started?** Read the beginner information or join the Telegram group. Businesses can find practical guidance on accepting Bitcoin.
- **How does the community stay independent?** Voluntary contributions help pay for the website, stickers, materials and activities. A contribution gives no influence over content, positions, speakers or activities.
- **How do I report a mistake or share an idea?** Email info@bitcoinfriesland.com with the page link and, ideally, a source we can check.

Times, locations, ticket prices and payment options at businesses can change. Check them with the organizer or business. A listing is not an endorsement.
