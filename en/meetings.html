Sunday, January 25, 2026 · 14:00 - 17:00

Bitcoin Friesland New Year's Drinks

Unia 12 - Uniabuurt 12, Leeuwarden

Bitcoin Friesland New Year's borrel. Unia 12 is a new Bitcoin-accepting spot in Leeuwarden and the perfect living-room setting to kick off the new year together and look back at everything we did with Bitcoin in 2025.
