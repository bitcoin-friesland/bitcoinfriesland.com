Our boundary

Our independence is not for sale

Financial or other support does not influence our content, opinions, advice, speakers or activities. We remain free to be critical of every party, including business supporters. A mention is not an endorsement.

Promotion must

  • ✓Be related to Bitcoin.
  • ✓Be agreed with the administrators in advance.
  • ✓Be honest and clear.
  • ✓Contain no spam, deception or dubious investments.

Where does the contribution go?

To community costs such as the website, stickers, materials and activities. We keep a simple record so it remains clear that contributions benefit Bitcoin Friesland.

Good to know

Will participation remain free?

Yes. Participation in Bitcoin Friesland and the Telegram group remains free for everyone.

Can only supporters share something?

No. Regular conversations, tips and knowledge sharing remain free. Supporter promotions require prior approval.

Can I already pay or order online?

Not yet. For now, we arrange payments and stickers personally. The shop and a permanent sats payment route will follow later.
