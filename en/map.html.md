# Bitcoin Map Friesland

The Bitcoin Map helps visitors find places in Friesland where Bitcoin or Lightning is accepted. The human-readable web page contains the current list and practical payment information.

## How to use it

- Search the business list by name, town or type. Clear the search to show every business again.

- Check the map or table to see which payment method a location supports.
- When in doubt, ask the business in advance whether Bitcoin payment is currently available.
- Tell Bitcoin Friesland about a missing or outdated location so the directory can be corrected.

A listing means that the location accepts or has accepted Bitcoin; it is not an endorsement of the business or its products.

- Current map: https://bitcoinfriesland.com/en/map
- Contact: info@bitcoinfriesland.com
